Jordan Del Bianco

LAT, ATC, LMT, CEAS III

HER STORY

Jordan didn't arrive at massage therapy by accident — she followed a thread that's woven through everything she does: a deep curiosity about how bodies work, how they break down, and how they come back to life.

Jordan has spent her career at the intersection of science and healing — driven by a single question: how do we help the body find its way back to itself?

With a BS in Athletic Training from the University of Delaware and over six years of clinical experience, Jordan has worked alongside an exceptionally diverse range of people — professional athletes, circus performers, collegiate and high school teams, industrial workers, and everyday movers. That range gave her something rare: the ability to meet any body where it is, without assumption or agenda.

Her expanded credentials — as a CEAS-III, Certified Holistic Health Coach, and Certified Holistic Nutritionist — reflect her belief that true recovery doesn't happen in isolation. Sleep, stress, movement, and nourishment are all part of the picture.

HER APPROACH

More than technique.
It's intentional care.

Jordan's approach is built on a simple belief: that the most effective healing draws from multiple traditions. Informed by Leslie Bruder's Phenomenal Touch philosophy, Jordan approaches each session as a fluid, three-dimensional experience rather than a series of isolated techniques. Depth, pressure, contact, and rhythm are balanced in real time, guided by deep listening to what each body communicates.

True recovery runs deeper than muscle and tissue — it moves through the nervous system, the breath, and the body's own capacity to heal. Somatic presence and nervous system regulation aren't peripheral to her work — they're woven into the core of it. That depth of understanding comes from years of training and real-world clinical experience across a wide range of disciplines.

Jordan brings the full spectrum of her expertise into every session — the clinical precision of athletic training, the environmental insight of ergonomics, the somatic depth of yoga, and the healing intelligence of massage — united in service of one thing: your body's ability to restore itself.

Away from the treatment room, you'll find Jordan exploring Colorado's trails, lifting weights, dancing, playing pickleball, skiing, and spending time with loved ones.

Services & Pricing

Every session at Balanced Beings is customized. The modalities below are tools Jordan draws from based on what your body needs that day.

MODALITIES

Sessions may incorporate any combination of the following:

Aromatherapy

The therapeutic use of essential oils to support relaxation, mood, and nervous system regulation, seamlessly integrated into your session.

Cupping

Suction-based therapy to lift soft tissue, improve blood flow, and release fascial restrictions.

Deep Tissue

Sustained pressure targeting deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue to release chronic tension.

Home Exercise Rx

Personalized movement and recovery programming to extend the benefits of your session beyond the table.

IASTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization — specialized tools to detect and treat scar tissue and myofascial restriction.

Kinesiology Taping

Elastic therapeutic tape applied to support muscles and joints, reduce inflammation, and enhance proprioception during activity and recovery.

Myofascial Release

Gentle, sustained pressure into the fascial connective tissue to eliminate pain and restore motion throughout the body.

Postural Assessment

A clinical evaluation of your movement patterns and alignment — identifying compensations and imbalances that may be contributing to pain or limiting performance.

Red Light Therapy

Low-level wavelengths of red light applied to the body to reduce inflammation, accelerate tissue repair, and support muscle recovery — a powerful complement to hands-on bodywork.

Sports Massage

Performance-focused work designed to support training, enhance recovery, and address sport-specific patterns.

Stretching & Flexibility Work

Assisted and active stretching techniques drawn from athletic training and yoga to improve range of motion, reduce tension, and support long-term mobility.

Swedish

Gentle, flowing strokes to promote relaxation, circulation, and overall wellbeing.

Thai Massage

Assisted stretching and rhythmic compression performed fully clothed to improve mobility and energy flow.

Trigger Point Therapy

Targeted pressure applied to hyperirritable spots in the muscle to release referred pain patterns, reduce tension, and restore normal function.

Injury-Focused Sessions

Who It's For
Injury-focused sessions are designed for anyone navigating a musculoskeletal injury, chronic pain, post-surgical recovery, or a movement limitation that has gone unaddressed. Whether you're an athlete working to return to sport, someone dealing with recurring tension or dysfunction, or a person who has simply been told to "just rest" without real answers — these sessions are built for you.

What to Expect
Your initial session begins with a thorough musculoskeletal evaluation drawing from Jordan's background as a Certified Athletic Trainer. Together, you'll discuss your history, movement patterns, and goals before Jordan conducts a hands-on assessment to identify the root cause of your symptoms — not just where it hurts, but why. Treatment follows immediately, integrating massage therapy, soft tissue mobilization, IASTM, and other relevant modalities. You'll leave with a personalized home care plan including targeted exercises and recovery strategies to support your progress between sessions.

What Makes It Different
Jordan treats the whole picture. Her training as both an Athletic Trainer and Licensed Massage Therapist gives her a clinical lens that few bodyworkers bring to the table — the ability to assess, diagnose movement dysfunction, and treat with precision while honoring the body's capacity to heal itself.
